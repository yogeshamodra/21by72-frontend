About 21BY72

Join Our Flagship Gathering

21BY72 is Bharat's leading startup summit, held in Surat - the birthplace of some of India's most consequential companies. Spend two days in candid conversations with the founders and investors backing bold ideas early, when conviction matters more than answers. Explore startup showcases spanning fintech, deep tech, healthcare and beyond, and walk away with real connections through curated investor-founder huddles. This is where ideas turn into companies that don't just scale, but solve meaningful problems and create lasting impact.
