About Us

Where conversations converge into companies

21BY72 is a startup summit in Surat, Gujarat. It brings founders, investors and ecosystem builders together for networking and funding. We want Surat to be known as The Startup City - on top of everything the city is already famous for.

The summit is organised by IVY Growth Associates, an angel network based in Surat, building an international corridor between startups and investors.

  • Exhibitions, live pitches and keynotes
  • Fireside chats, panels and workshops
  • Curated investor-founder huddles
